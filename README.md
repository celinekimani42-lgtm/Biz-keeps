Think of BIZ KEEP as the Ultimate High-Tech Notebook for Running a Shop in Kenya 🇰🇪
1. No More Lost Paper Notebooks
Imagine you run a busy shop in Nairobi selling sneakers, snacks, or phone accessories. Usually, shopkeepers write down sales in messy exercise books that can easily get lost or stained with chai. BIZ KEEP is a clean website that replaces those paper books with a smart digital dashboard on a phone or laptop.

2. Tracking Every Single Shilling (M-PESA & Cash)
In Kenya, customers pay in lots of ways—some tap their phone with M-PESA Till (Buy Goods), some send money to a Paybill, others swipe a bank card, and some hand over hard cash. BIZ KEEP logs every payment in one place instantly, so at the end of the day, you know exactly how much cash you actually made without having to count receipts one by one.

3. Instant Receipts That KRA Loves (eTIMS)
Whenever you buy something at a supermarket, you get a printed slip. In Kenya, the government tax office (KRA) requires businesses to provide official tax receipts called eTIMS. BIZ KEEP does the math automatically: it calculates the 16% tax, adds an official QR code, and lets you print a mini receipt or send it straight to the customer's phone via WhatsApp or SMS in two clicks.

4. The "Who Owes Me Money?" Tracker (Madeni)
Every local business has loyal customers who say, "Let me take this today and I’ll pay next week" (madeni or Lipa Pole Pole). If you don't write it down, you lose money. BIZ KEEP keeps a friendly list of who owes you, how much they’ve paid back so far, and can even send them a gentle text message reminder when payment day arrives.

5. A Safe Locker & Growth Report Card
Just like your school keeps your report cards and birth certificates safe, BIZ KEEP has a digital document vault to store important business papers (like trade licenses and tax permits). Plus, whenever the owner wants to ask a bank or SACCO for a business loan to expand, the app can generate a neat 1-page financial report that proves the business is making healthy profits
